How to use Pulse & Pitch

Made for the music stand.

On iPhone, open in Safari. Choose Share → Add to Home Screen for an app-like experience. After the first online load, the app works offline.

Keep the app in the foreground and the phone unlocked. We request screen wake lock where available; iOS can still interrupt audio. Start sound or microphone with a tap.

For tuning, play one sustained note at a moderate volume, with the microphone a little away from the bell. ±5 cents is marked in tune. The frequency always shows the actual sounding pitch; the note name follows your transposition setting. The metronome stops when you switch to the tuner.

Some Bluetooth audio devices add latency or change microphone quality. The built-in microphone and speaker are the best starting point. Headphones help in a noisy room. This is a practice aid, not a laboratory reference.
